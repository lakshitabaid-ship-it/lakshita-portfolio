About me

A little about me

For me, communication is the thread that ties everything together. It started on stage, through debates, leadership programmes, national-level representation and idea-pitching.

What fascinates me most is the space where marketing, communication, business and entrepreneurship meet: how brands build relationships, how communities grow, and how an idea travels from a conversation to something people can actually use.

I want to build things, work with interesting people, and eventually build something of my own. I'm not following a conventional career path, and I'm okay with that.
